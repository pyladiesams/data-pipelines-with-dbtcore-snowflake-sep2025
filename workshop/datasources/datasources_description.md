# Art gallery data

During this workshop we will be using a set of realistic, simulated data sources that model the operations of a small to medium-sized art gallery business with multiple locations. 

The core dataset includes detailed records of over 5,000 **artwork sales transactions** spanning three years, capturing information such as sale date, price, commission, customer, and gallery location. 

Supporting tables provide a comprehensive view of the gallery ecosystem: 
 - **artwork inventory** lists all available and sold pieces
 - **artist profiles** detail the backgrounds and tiers of represented artists
 - **customer data** offers anonymised information about buyers. 
 
In addition, reference data ([“seed files”](workshop/datasources/seeds)) define **artwork categories**, **gallery locations**, and standard **commission rates** by artist tier.
